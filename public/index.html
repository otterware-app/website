Your agents, your accounts

Claude Code and Codex on your Mac, OpenClaw and Hermes on your own gateway. Otter connects to them; it doesn't replace them.

Right where you are

⌘I opens a chat over the mail in front of you, and that mail is its context. Tabs keep what it read and made beside the chat. No copying, no pasting.

Nothing sent without your yes

Supervised by default: your agent asks before it sends, deletes or changes a mailbox, and shows you exactly what it will do.

Tools across every module

Search, read, file and draft mail; add and answer events; publish documents to Otter Drive. Plugins bring their own tools, so agents grow with your Otter.

For any agent on your Mac

Cursor, a terminal's Claude Code, anything that speaks MCP gets Otter's tools with a token, read only, safe or full access.

One plane, every device

Every chat, whatever agent ran it, reads the same on every device. OpenClaw, Hermes and OpenRouter chats carry on from any of them; Claude Code and Codex on their Mac.

Scheduled

Your agents work while you don't: a morning brief, an inbox sweep at noon, Friday's report. Scheduled is where all of it lands, each run in its own chat, named for the job and the day, with a notification when it's in.

Yours, on your devices

Otter reads your mail and calendars straight from Gmail, Outlook or your IMAP server, on your device, and keeps its cache there. Your agents run on your own accounts and gateways. An Otter account syncs your mailboxes, preferences and chats, and wakes your devices when mail arrives. If you use the OpenRouter agent, its chats and what its tools read are stored on our server. No analytics, no tracking. Details are in the privacy policy, and the code is open source under the MIT license.
