Your mail stays yours

Otter reads your mail from Gmail, Outlook or your IMAP server directly on your device and keeps its mailbox cache there. An Otter account, optional on the Mac, syncs your mailboxes and preferences, keeps your agents and their chats on our server so every device shows the same ones, and wakes your devices when mail arrives. No analytics, no tracking. Details are in the privacy policy, and the code is open source under the MIT license.
