Your mail stays yours

Otter Mail reads your mail from Gmail, Outlook or your IMAP server directly on your device and keeps its mailbox cache there. An Otter account, optional on the Mac, syncs your mailboxes, preferences and agent chats and wakes your devices when mail arrives. If you use the OpenRouter agent, its chat history and mail returned by tools are stored on our server and sent to OpenRouter and the selected model provider. No analytics, no tracking. Details are in the privacy policy, and the code is open source under the MIT license.
