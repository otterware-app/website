Coming to Agents

Scheduled

Your agents work while you don't: a morning brief, an inbox sweep at noon, Friday's report, a check on the build. Scheduled is where it all lands.

Each run arrives in its own chat, named for the job and the day, with a notification when it's in, and a header that says what ran it. A run that needs you brings its chat back to the top.

It already works with OpenClaw: install Otter's channel on your gateway, and what a scheduled job announces comes to Otter. A Scheduled view in Agents brings every run, from every agent, into one place.

  • Scheduled · Inbox sweep12:00 PM
    Archived 14 newsletters Kept the two you usually read. Nothing needed a reply.
  • Scheduled · Build watch3:30 PM

  • Sent by Rowan4:12 PM

Pointers, not your content

Claude Code and Codex run on your Mac; OpenClaw and Hermes on your own gateway, and Otter talks to them directly. When OpenClaw's channel tells your devices something new has arrived, our server carries only which agent and which chat, never the text. The OpenRouter agent is the exception: it runs on our server, which keeps its chats and what its tools read. Details are in the privacy policy.
